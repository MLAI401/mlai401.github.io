## 📊 Core Curricula & Topics

Our materials are divided into four main pillars, taking you from classical statistical modeling to the cutting edge of AI:

### 1. Foundational Machine Learning
The bedrock of ML, focusing on statistical learning theory and classic algorithms.
- **Linear & Logistic Regression**: Gradient descent, regularization (L2/L1), and optimization.
- **Supervised Learning**: Support Vector Machines (SVMs), Decision Trees, and Random Forests.
- **Unsupervised Learning**: K-Means clustering, Principal Component Analysis (PCA), and anomaly detection.
- **Evaluation**: ROC-AUC, precision-recall, cross-validation, and error analysis.

### 2. Deep Learning
Neural network architectures and deep representation learning.
- **Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs)**: Forward propagation, backpropagation, and activation functions (ReLU, GELU, Sigmoid).
- **Computer Vision**: Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Pooling, Residual Networks (ResNets).
- **Sequence Modeling**: Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), and Attention mechanisms.
- **Optimization**: Adam, RMSprop, learning rate schedules, and batch normalization.

### 3. Reinforcement Learning
Learning to make decisions by interacting with an environment.
- **Tabular RL**: Markov Decision Processes (MDPs), Q-Learning, and SARSA.
- **Deep RL**: Deep Q-Networks (DQN), Policy Gradients (REINFORCE), and Actor-Critic models.
- **Exploration vs. Exploitation**: $\epsilon$-greedy strategies and Upper Confidence Bounds (UCB).

### 4. Generative AI & Large Language Models
The modern frontier of generative modeling and language understanding.
- **Generative Modeling**: Autoencoders, Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), and Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs).
- **Transformers**: Multi-head self-attention, encoder-decoder architectures, and positional encodings.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs)**: Pre-training, fine-tuning, LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation), and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
